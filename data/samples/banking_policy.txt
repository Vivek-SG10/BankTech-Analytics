BANKTECH ANALYTICS — BANKING POLICY DOCUMENT

=======================================================
1. ACCOUNT OPENING ELIGIBILITY
=======================================================

BankTech Analytics welcomes individuals and entities who meet our general eligibility criteria
for account opening. Eligibility is assessed based on factors including age, residency status,
and the purpose of the account. Accounts may be opened by resident individuals, joint applicants,
minors (through guardians), sole proprietors, partnerships, private limited companies, public
limited companies, trusts, associations, and other permissible entities as per applicable law.

All applicants are subject to our internal customer due diligence process. We reserve the right
to decline an account application without providing a reason, in accordance with our risk
management and regulatory obligations.

=======================================================
2. DOCUMENT REQUIREMENTS FOR ACCOUNT OPENING
=======================================================

All account applicants must furnish documents satisfying Know Your Customer (KYC) norms as
prescribed by the Reserve Bank of India and other relevant authorities. The required documents
generally fall into two categories:

Proof of Identity: Government-issued photo identity documents such as passport, Aadhaar card
(linked to a registered mobile number), Permanent Account Number (PAN) card, voter identity
card, or driving licence are accepted.

Proof of Address: Recent utility bills, bank statements, government-issued address proofs, or
any other document accepted as valid address proof under the KYC Master Direction may be
submitted.

For business accounts, additional documentation such as certificates of incorporation, memoranda
of association, board resolutions, partnership deeds, GST registration certificates, and lists
of authorised signatories may be required.

Failure to furnish complete and valid documentation will result in the account application being
placed on hold until the deficiency is resolved.

=======================================================
3. GENERAL LOAN ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA
=======================================================

BankTech Analytics offers a range of loan products including personal loans, home loans, vehicle
loans, business loans, and educational loans. All loan applications are subject to credit
assessment and internal underwriting policies.

Loan eligibility is determined by, but is not limited to, the following factors:
   - Creditworthiness of the applicant as assessed by recognised credit bureaus
   - Repayment capacity based on income, existing liabilities, and financial commitments
   - Nature, purpose, and tenure of the requested loan
   - Collateral or security offered, where applicable

All lending decisions are subject to credit assessment by authorised underwriting personnel.
Loan approvals, amounts, and terms are at the sole discretion of BankTech Analytics and may
differ from amounts applied for. Meeting the minimum eligibility criteria does not guarantee
approval of a loan application.

=======================================================
4. PRIVACY POLICY SUMMARY
=======================================================

BankTech Analytics is committed to safeguarding the privacy and confidentiality of customer
information. Personal and financial data collected from customers is used exclusively for the
purposes of account servicing, regulatory compliance, fraud prevention, and improvement of
our products and services.

Customer data will not be shared with unaffiliated third parties without explicit consent,
except where required by law, court order, or regulatory directive. We maintain technical,
administrative, and physical safeguards to protect customer information from unauthorised
access, disclosure, or misuse.

Customers have the right to access their personal data held by us and to request correction
of any inaccuracies. For data-related requests, please contact our Data Protection Officer
through official channels listed on our website.

=======================================================
5. GRIEVANCE REDRESSAL
=======================================================

BankTech Analytics has established a structured grievance redressal mechanism in compliance
with regulatory requirements. Any customer who is dissatisfied with our products, services,
or the conduct of our staff is encouraged to raise a complaint through official channels.

Complaints may be submitted via our official website, mobile banking application, branch
offices, or customer care helpline. All complaints are assigned a unique reference number
and acknowledged promptly. We aim to resolve grievances within the timeframe prescribed by
applicable regulations.

If a customer remains unsatisfied after exhausting our internal redressal process, they may
escalate the matter to the relevant banking ombudsman or regulatory authority. Details of
escalation channels are available on our official website and at all branch offices.

BankTech Analytics treats all customer complaints seriously and uses feedback to continuously
improve service delivery and operational standards.

# Banking Compliance & Regulatory Guidelines (FAQ)

## 1. Anti-Money Laundering (AML) Regulations
### What is the threshold for mandatory Currency Transaction Reports (CTRs)?
Financial institutions are required to file a CTR for each deposit, withdrawal, exchange of currency, or other payment or transfer by, through, or to the institution that involves a transaction in currency of more than **$10,000**.

### What constitutes suspicious activity under SAR guidelines?
A Suspicious Activity Report (SAR) must be filed when a transaction involves or aggregates at least **$5,000**, and the bank knows, suspects, or has reason to suspect that:
1. The transaction involves funds derived from illegal activities or is intended to hide funds.
2. The transaction is designed to evade Bank Secrecy Act (BSA) requirements (e.g., structuring).
3. The transaction has no business or apparent lawful purpose.

## 2. Know Your Customer (KYC) Standards
### What are the core pillars of a Customer Due Diligence (CDD) rule?
1. Customer identification and verification.
2. Beneficial ownership identification and verification (25% or more equity interest).
3. Understanding the nature and purpose of customer relationships to develop customer risk profiles.
4. Ongoing monitoring for reporting suspicious transactions and maintaining customer information.

## 3. Fraud Prevention & Transaction Monitoring
### How should high-risk velocity alerts be handled?
High-velocity transactions (e.g., multiple transfers within 10 minutes exceeding usual baseline limits) should trigger automated temporary holds pending two-factor authentication or manual fraud team clearance.
